# Privacy

AIspy collects no data.

The plugin is instructions, templates and examples only. It has no server, no telemetry or tracking,
and runs no code of its own.

A review runs in your own Claude session. During it, Claude visits the site you asked it to review
and the benchmark sites it researches, through the browser and search tools in your session. The
captures, notes and documents it makes are saved only in the folder you choose.

Questions: https://github.com/stevensmith-design/aispy/issues
