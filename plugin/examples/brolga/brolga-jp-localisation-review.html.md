<div class="f-head"><div class="f-no">1</div><div>
<h3>Pages are wider than the phone: the welcome ×, size guide and sticky-bar button sit off-screen</h3>
<div class="f-tags"><span class="tag">Visual <small>ビジュアル</small></span><span class="tag prio high">High</span><span class="tag">Goal: mobile conversion, trust</span></div>

<div class="f-body">

<p class="why"><b>Why it matters.</b> On Swell 3, 68% of visible characters are 13 px and 20% are 12 px; the 15 px base size carries 5.5%. The delivery, returns and payment lines, the review sizes and the size numbers are decision information read at arm's length, often outdoors, and older runners are in scope. <div class="f-head"><div class="f-no">4</div><div>
<h3>Japanese text breaks mid-word, from the first screen to the width label</h3>
<div class="f-tags"><span class="tag">Visual <small>ビジュアル</small></span><span class="tag prio mid">Medium</span><span class="tag">Goal: trust</span></div>
</div></div>
<div class="f-body">
<p class="where">Top page, /shoes/, product pages, footer · phone</p>
<p class="why"><b>Why it matters.</b> Japanese has no spaces, so browsers break anywhere unless told otherwise. A Japanese reader notices a split word instantly; it is one of the clearest signs of a site that was translated rather than written for them, which works against the second goal. Product names are Latin and don't break, so the most visible cases are the hero and the width label itself.</p>
<ul class="covers">
<li>The top page's first-screen lede splits 「オー｜ストラリア」.</li>
<li>The /shoes/ lead splits the width label: 「（4E相｜当）」.</li>
<li>Card use lines split 「搭｜載」, 「イー｜ジーラン」 and 「クッ｜ション」, which also misaligns the card rows.</li>
<li>The compare table splits 「ロ｜ング走」 and 「テ｜ンポ走」; a review date splits 「11｜日」.</li>
</ul>
<div class="shots">

<h3>Finding a model</h3>

<article class="finding" id="f5" data-key="width-lost-between-pages">
<div class="f-head"><div class="f-no">5</div><div>
<h3>The chosen width is lost: product pages open on 標準, and filters vanish on back</h3>
<div class="f-tags"><span class="tag">IA <small>情報設計</small></span><span class="tag prio high">High</span><span class="tag">Goal: mobile conversion</span></div>
</div></div>
<div class="f-body">
<p class="where">/shoes/ → every product page · phone and desktop</p>
<p class="why"><b>Why it matters.</b> The brand's audience arrives saying "wide": from search, from the menu's ワイド（4E相当）, from the filter. The product page answers "standard", and on a phone the width toggle sits above the size grid where a runner going straight to their size may not notice it. That is the exact wrong-width order the brand exists to prevent, and Brolga pays the exchange shipping both ways.</p>
<ul class="covers">
<li>Cards in the wide list link to the bare product URL; Swell 3 opens with 標準（2E相当） checked, and <code>?width=wide</code> is ignored.</li>
<li>Filters and sort aren't in the URL: after filtering ワイド + 27.0 (7件), opening a shoe and going back shows all 10 shoes in default order.</li>
<li>The use chips drop the width: from <code>/shoes/?width=wide</code>, ロード goes to <code>?use=road</code>.</li>
</ul>
<div class="shots">
<figure><img data-img="19" data-src="img/f5-filtered-a@2x.png" width="390" height="166" style="width:390px" alt="Listing filtered to wide and 27.0, 7 results"><figcaption><span class="mk">5</span> Filtered: 7件, ワイド（4E相当） and 27.0. The URL stays <code>/shoes/</code>.</figcaption></figure>

</div>
<div class="ref"><h4>DONE RIGHT IN JAPAN</h4>
<ul><li><b>HOKA</b> keeps width as a variant of one product and carries it in the URL (<code>width=Wide</code>), with tiles named 「Clifton 11 — ワイド」.</li>
<li><b>ASICS</b> and <b>Mizuno</b> make wide a separate product (EXTRA WIDE, SW), so the width can't be lost. <span class="not">Not to copy: separate products. Brolga's one-page selector is better for comparing the two fits; carry the width instead.</span></li></ul>
</div>
<div class="prop"><h4>PROPOSAL</h4>
<ul><li>Cards in a width-filtered list link with <code>?width=wide</code>; the product page reads it, and writes it back when the toggle changes.</li>
